[h1]Heavy Horde[/h1]
More zombies, and tougher ones. Made so fights stay dangerous even with the Boltgun.

[h2]Tougher zombies[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Mutated[/b] (15%): glowing mutants with more health, armour, harder hits and a bit more speed.
[*][b]Brute[/b] (5%): heavy mutants that regenerate and keep their limbs. One hit can only take 40% of their health, so even a Boltgun needs about 3 bolts.
[*][b]Behemoth[/b] (1%): giants with a boss health bar. About 7 Boltgun bolts.
[/list]

[h2]More zombies[/h2]
[list]
[*]Bigger zombie groups in newly explored areas.
[*]The game's own ambushes come more often, twice as big, and more often with the toughest zombies around.
[*]Hunts: an extra ambush every few minutes.
[/list]

Everything is adjustable in BepInEx\config\girthy_mods.zedzone.heavyhorde.cfg.

[h2]Co-op[/h2]
Works when the host has it: the host spawns the zombies and owns their health, and guests see the same variants. Install it on every PC anyway.

Requires BepInEx (IL2CPP), like other ZED ZONE code mods.
